# 서울 아파트 시장: 가격 수준, 밸류에이션, 정책·신용·공급 환경과 15억 이하 매수자에게 주는 의미 (기준일 2026-11-12)

Notes on scope and conventions: every figure is labeled with its as-of date. "R-ONE" = 한국부동산원 (Korea Real Estate Board) survey index. "KB" = KB부동산 monthly/weekly survey. 2021–2025 figures are **historical comparison**. Per-3.3㎡ prices from MOLIT 실거래 aggregators are on an **전용면적 (exclusive-area) basis**, so they run roughly 20–25% higher than "평당가" quoted on a 공급면적 basis. The direct site www.reb.or.kr could not be reached from this session (network block), so R-ONE numbers come from news coverage of its releases and from data mirrors.

## 1. Price level and trend: R-ONE weekly/monthly, 2026 YTD, KB average/median and 3.3㎡ prices, 2025 full year, and comparison with the 2021–22 peak

### Takeaway
Seoul apartment prices are at record nominal highs. R-ONE has risen 92 weeks in a row, and its 2026 year-to-date gain of about 8.8% (as of Nov 2) already beats every year since 2012. KB's figure is higher at 10.89% through October. Momentum is clearly fading, though: the weekly gain fell from about 0.2% in Aug–Sep to 0.07% in early November. 강남3구 has fallen for 10–14 weeks in a row, and 성동·강동 turned negative on Nov 9. Prices are well above the 2021–22 nominal peak, but on an inflation-adjusted basis they are only about back to that peak, and several outer districts (노도강·금천) were still below their old index peaks as of late August 2026.

**Data-quality caveats**
- Survey sources disagree. For October cumulative changes: 서초 −1.21% (R-ONE) vs −0.01% (KB) vs +0.24% (R114); 송파 −0.75% vs +0.29% vs +0.11%; 강남 −1.38% vs −0.29% vs +0.03% — [경제일보](https://www.kyungjeilbo.com/view/20261108150711639)
- R-ONE's weekly sample can show gains where there were no trades (e.g., 성북 +0.3% in a week when large complexes recorded no sales). MOLIT has shelved its planned overhaul of the weekly statistics — [한국경제TV](https://www.wowtv.co.kr/NewsCenter/News/Read?articleId=A202611050122)
- The average 실거래 price per 3.3㎡ fell from 5,548만 (May) to 5,045만 (Aug) because high-end transactions shrank, a composition effect rather than a broad price fall — [스트레이트뉴스 (한국도시연구소)](https://www.straightnews.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=314954)

### Inferences
- If the weekly pace holds at 0.03–0.07% for the remaining ~7 releases, R-ONE's 2026 total would end around +9.0–9.3%, a second consecutive record year. KB will likely end at 11–12%. Both are extrapolations, not forecasts.
- This is a two-speed market. The high end (강남3구, then 성동·강동) is correcting under tax, rate and loan-cap pressure, while the lower and middle tiers are still rising but decelerating. The leadership rotation from 2025 (한강벨트) to 2026 (외곽) is a classic "키 맞추기".
- Nominal prices are at all-time highs. In real terms Seoul is only around its Dec 2021 peak: roughly −3.7% in Jun 2026, and given KB's further ~4% nominal gain Jul–Oct and CPI near 3%, now plausibly within about ±1% of it. That is a rough estimate, not a published figure.

## 2. District table (25 자치구): 3.3㎡ price, 2026 YTD change and 전세가율; who led and who lagged ("키 맞추기")

### Takeaway
2026 leadership flipped completely from 2025. 성북 (+16.56% YTD), 서대문, 강서, 구로, 노원, 동대문, 관악 and 강북 rose double digits, while 강남 (−2.02%) and 서초 (−0.23%) are down year to date and 송파 is up only about 4%. This is a textbook catch-up rally ("키 맞추기/갭 메우기") by cheaper districts after 2025's 강남·한강벨트 surge. The price gap per 3.3㎡ is still about 3.9x, from roughly 1.1억 in 강남 to about 2,850만 in 도봉.

## 3. Valuation metrics: PIR, K-HAI, 전세가율, rental yield vs bond and mortgage rates, published over/undervaluation estimates, international comparison

### Takeaway
On income-based and yield-based measures Seoul looks expensive. The housing-burden index K-HAI is 185.3 (Q2 2026), meaning a median household spends about 47.6% of income on mortgage payments; that is the highest since Q4 2022 but below the 2022 Q3 peak of 214.6. ## 4. Credit and rates: BOK base rate and its 2026 path, mortgage rate range, stress-DSR stage, household-debt controls

### Inferences

## 5. Policy in force (Nov 2026): 10.15 대책, 토허 expiry, tax changes, supply plans, and Seoul policy after the June 2026 local elections

### Takeaway
All core 10.15 measures remain in force: Seoul-wide 조정대상지역 and 투기과열지구, Seoul-wide 토허 with a 2-year owner-occupancy duty (blocking gap investment), the 6/4/2억 loan caps, and LTV 40%. ### Inferences
- The key near-term policy event for a ≤15억 buyer is the decision, due before Dec 31, on extending Seoul-wide 토허. Extension would keep gap investment blocked and demand limited to owner-occupiers, sustaining the 전세 shortage. Lifting it, fully or partly (e.g., outside 강남3구 and 용산), could revive leveraged gap buying in high-전세가율 outer districts, which is exactly the segment where gap investment drove 2020–21. The minister's Oct 7 comments lean toward extension, but her Nov 12 "falling prices" narrative leaves room for a partial adjustment. This is a judgment call; there is no announcement yet.
- For a single owner-occupied home bought at or below 15억, the Aug 3 package is neutral to positive: no 종부세 below about 20억 market value, and larger long-residence capital-gains deductions. The burden falls on non-resident owners and homes above 30–40억. The 2027–28 eased 다주택 surcharge is designed to bring listings to market, a bearish supply factor for 2027.

### Gaps
- No official statement on extending or lifting the 토허 designation after 2026-12-31 was found as of Nov 12.
- I found no 2026 change to 취득세 rates (not specifically searched). Statutory 1주택 rates are 1% (≤6억), sliding 1–3% (6–9억) and 3% (>9억), plus surtaxes; these were not re-verified here.
- Final passage of the 종부세/양도세 bills is expected around late November 2026.

## 6. Supply and demand: 입주물량 2025–27, 인허가·착공, monthly 거래량, 매매수급·buyer sentiment, 미분양

## 8. The ≤15억 segment: is the 15억 loan threshold concentrating demand just under 15억, and how did lower- and mid-priced areas move in 2026?

### Takeaway
Yes. The 10.15 notch (a 6억 loan at or below 15억, 4억 above) produced measurable bunching. The share of Seoul trades at or below 15억 jumped from about 65% to 80–87% after 10.15. A peer-reviewed DID study finds transactions just below 15억 rose 19.6–31.4% and unit prices 7.0%, versus 4.0% for cheaper bands. Homes at 13억 have been repricing toward 15억 ("15억 키 맞추기"). In 2026 the low- and mid-priced districts (6–12억 for 84㎡급) led Seoul with +11–17% YTD on R-ONE. Since August, though, the 9–15억 band's share has shrunk relative to sub-9억, because high LTV is only available to 생애최초 and policy-loan buyers and 9억+ homes need 5억+ of cash.

### Cited Findings
- Share of Seoul apartment trades at or below 15억: **64.6%** (Oct 16–31, 2025) → **73.2%** (Nov) → **81.5%** (Dec 2025) → 80.2% (Jan 2026) → **87.2%** (Feb 1–17, 2026, partial month). ### Inferences
- The 15억 notch has created a policy-made price ceiling-and-magnet. Homes just under 15억 carry a premium, about 3%p more unit-price gain than cheaper bands per the KCI study, and assets at 13–14억 have tended to converge toward 15억. A ≤15억 buyer is effectively competing in the most crowded, most loan-dependent part of the market, and pays a "notch premium" there.
- That segment is also the most rate- and credit-sensitive: highest loan-to-price ratios, mostly variable-rate loans, and BOK flagging it as the main source of new mortgage demand. A November hike, a 2027 loan-quota squeeze, or a policy change on the 15억 line would hit it first. Conversely, if 토허 is lifted, the 2020–21 precedent suggests gap investment would re-enter exactly this band, with high 전세가율 outer districts most exposed.
- Sub-band dynamics matter for a buyer. Since August, sub-9억 (policy-loan and 생애최초 LTV 70% territory) has been absorbing share from 9–15억, where the 40% LTV forces 5–9억 of cash. Within ≤15억, homes in the 13–15억 range in 한강벨트-adjacent districts (e.g., 84㎡ in 동작·마포·영등포·강동·중구·종로, which average 13.7–16.3억) are close to or above the threshold. Most 84㎡급 stock in 노도강·금관구·중랑·은평·성북·강서·서대문·동대문·양천 averages 6–12억.

### Gaps
- No 2026 dataset was found that gives the exact share of Seoul trades in the 14억–15억 band specifically (vs 15–16억) after 10.15. The KCI study's band definitions (the width of the "near" band) were not visible in the abstract.
- No data on how 2026 price gains split between 13–15억 and sub-9억 homes within the same districts.
